⌖ FLIGHT CONTROLS

Tap the left or right half of the screen to change lanes. On a keyboard, use ← / → or A / D. Tap the pause icon or press P to pause. Titans launch rockets with the lower-left button or F; Falcons activate phase with that button or G.

✦ THE STARFRONT STORY

Star Gems are the living fuel of the galaxy. The Star Eater's raider fleets have stolen them from five star systems to power a machine that will drain every sun. Recovering gems weakens the invaders and funds the ships and technology needed to push them back. Gem Runs gather the resources; Starfront War takes the fight to the enemy.

🎯 MISSION OBJECTIVES

Avoid incoming red hazards. Collect crystals to score, grow your combo, and bank gems after each run. Collected power-ups activate automatically. Complete daily challenges and achievements, then claim their rewards from the Rewards page.

⚡ POWER-UP SYSTEMS

⬡ShieldAbsorbs one collision (two with Shield 2.0).
⌁MagnetPulls nearby gems toward your fighter.
◷Slow timeSlows hazards for a short time.
ϟLaserAutomatically fires at the next hazard ahead. Twin Laser targets two hazards.
➤Titan rocketsManually destroy the nearest block ahead. Rockets are limited per flight; Guided Rack adds extra ammunition.

🚀 FIGHTER ABILITIES

DARTGHOSTTITANFALCON

Dart is balanced. Ghost unlocks at combo x10 (27 consecutive gems), has a smaller hitbox, and doubles gem rewards at 1,200 speed. Titan unlocks at combo x15 (42 consecutive gems), starts shielded, and fires manual rockets. Falcon can phase through hazards once per flight for five seconds; phase capacitors in the market extend it. Mirage auto-cloaks, Comet boosts gem score, Tempest auto-fires lasers, and each helicopter has a distinct support, armor, or evasion perk.

◉ GAME MODES

Gem Run contains Survival, a 60-second Time Attack, no-hazard Free Flight, and five sequential story campaigns. Every campaign has five escalating runs; gather Star Gems to damage each guardian, and reach the supreme monster in run five. Clearing all five worlds opens the next campaign tier. Starfront War is a touch-controlled space shooter across five star systems and 30 sequential missions per tier.

✦ HANGAR & REWARDS

Use My Hangar to review your fighters, skins, technologies, and power-up upgrades. Choose a free male or female pilot portrait in the Market, then change your callsign and equip portraits you own here. Your pilot rank grows with both your total flight score and combat rating. A glowing Rewards button means one or more rewards are ready to claim.

⚔ STARFRONT WAR COMBAT

Drag your ship anywhere on screen while its cannons fire automatically. Each sortie starts with four lives and has two life pickups dropped by enemies during its waves. Collect pickups by flying into them, including between waves. A mission has three to seven escalating waves; alien raiders weave in formations, gunships unleash aimed bursts, and kamikaze craft dive at your position. System finale bosses use different attack patterns and weapon types; watch the health bar and remaining HP above the battlefield. Every completed mission banks its gem reward and shows your sortie stats. Clear all 30 missions to unlock the next tier; open it yourself from the Warfront Map when you are ready.

ϟRapid FireFires your cannons faster for 8 seconds.
✣Spread ShotFires three lanes of shots at once.
✦Star GemsAdds 5 gems to your mission payout.
